# Governance, Operations and Measurement

## Governance
Brand changes follow propose, evidence-check, cross-functional review, approval, publish, adoption, measurement and deprecation.

## Trust controls
Claims substantiation, intellectual-property rights, accessibility, responsible AI, privacy/security language and correction/crisis procedures are mandatory.

## Operations
Use a versioned manifest, token registry, asset taxonomy, template library, training programme, exception register and deprecation policy.

## KPI tree
- **Awareness:** Share of search, direct traffic, aided/unaided awareness and quality reach (Quarterly). Decision: Do target users recognise Axiom and its category?
- **Distinctiveness:** Correct asset attribution, visual/verbal recognition and message recall (Biannual). Decision: Can people identify Axiom without the logo?
- **Consideration:** Qualified visits, comparison inclusion, demo/start intent and buyer engagement (Monthly). Decision: Does the brand enter serious evaluation?
- **Trust:** Governance proof consumption, objection resolution and trust survey (Monthly/Quarterly). Decision: Do users believe Axiom is controllable and credible?
- **Experience:** Activation, task success, time-to-value, recovery success and NPS/CSAT (Weekly/Monthly). Decision: Does product behaviour deliver the promise?
- **Content authority:** Qualified organic traffic, saves, citations, completion and assisted pipeline (Monthly). Decision: Is Axiom becoming a trusted source?
- **Advocacy:** Referrals, community contribution, reviews and case-study participation (Quarterly). Decision: Will users publicly recommend and teach others?
- **Operational consistency:** Template adoption, token compliance, exception volume and asset freshness (Monthly). Decision: Can the organisation execute the brand reliably?
- **Commercial impact:** Brand-assisted pipeline, conversion quality, retention and expansion (Quarterly). Decision: Is brand strength improving business outcomes?
