# 26-Week Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Brand Control Baseline (Weeks 1-2)
- **Focus:** Inventory existing decisions, establish authority, lock versioning and name owners.
- **Outputs:** Brand charter; inventory; risk register; source-of-truth decision.
- **Gate:** G0

## Phase 1 — Research & Strategic Core (Weeks 2-5)
- **Focus:** Audience research, category audit, purpose, promise, values and brand enemy.
- **Outputs:** Evidence repository; strategy foundation; audience priority.
- **Gate:** G1-G3

## Phase 2 — Positioning & Architecture (Weeks 5-8)
- **Focus:** Category, positioning, differentiation, proof, naming and product architecture.
- **Outputs:** Positioning platform; naming grammar; proof register.
- **Gate:** G4-G5

## Phase 3 — Narrative & Verbal System (Weeks 8-11)
- **Focus:** Master story, message house, audience propositions, voice, tone and microcopy.
- **Outputs:** Narrative system; copy bank; claims library.
- **Gate:** G6-G7

## Phase 4 — Visual & Dynamic Identity (Weeks 10-15)
- **Focus:** Logo refinement, colour, typography, layout, icons, imagery, motion and accessibility.
- **Outputs:** Identity system; tokens; templates; motion specification.
- **Gate:** G8-G9

## Phase 5 — Experience & Channel Design (Weeks 13-18)
- **Focus:** Product experience principles, website architecture, docs, social and lifecycle.
- **Outputs:** Experience contract; channel matrix; website/content briefs.
- **Gate:** G10-G11

## Phase 6 — Content, Launch & Enablement (Weeks 17-22)
- **Focus:** Editorial engine, launch campaign, press, sales assets, community and training.
- **Outputs:** Launch kit; content calendar; press kit; enablement library.
- **Gate:** G12-G17

## Phase 7 — Measurement & Evolution (Weeks 22-26 and ongoing)
- **Focus:** Baseline metrics, dashboards, quarterly reviews, annual audits and controlled evolution.
- **Outputs:** KPI tree; dashboard; review cadence; change backlog.
- **Gate:** G18


## Rollout rule
Do not launch as a big-bang cosmetic change. Lock authority and evidence, pilot controlled surfaces, validate comprehension and accessibility, train contributors, migrate priority assets and deprecate old assets with a deadline.
