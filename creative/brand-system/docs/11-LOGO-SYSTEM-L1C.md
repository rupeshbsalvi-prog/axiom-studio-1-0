# Logo System L1C — Precision Ribbon A

Status: **candidate; visual approval required**

L1C replaces L1B as the active candidate direction without deleting earlier
exploration. It separates responsive identity from illustrative Studio imagery.

## Variants

- **Signature:** high-detail transparent SVG for hero, launch and motion use.
- **Lockup:** signature plus manually outlined `AXIOM STUDIO` lettering.
- **App icon:** contained graphite application tile.
- **Micro:** four-colour, filter-free mark for 16–64 px UI use.

## Motion contract

The signature exposes stable group IDs:

1. `ribbon-left` — rises and resolves into the left structural leg.
2. `ribbon-crown` — folds over the apex.
3. `ribbon-right` — sweeps down to complete the monogram.
4. `prism-core` — pulses once after assembly.
5. `energy-orbits` — carries intelligence signals through the mark.
6. `surface-highlights` — provides one restrained final shimmer.

Motion must honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; the final static frame is always a
complete logo. No part may loop continuously beside reading content.

## Usage boundary

The signature is not a 16 px icon. Responsive selection must use the app icon
or micro mark at smaller sizes. Studio icon-family production remains gated on
approval of this masterbrand direction.
