# Visual, Motion and Experience System

## Core idea: Precision Prism
One clear idea enters an intelligent structure and emerges as coordinated multi-disciplinary output. Crystal-glass geometry, controlled spectral accents, editorial typography and structural grids communicate imagination under direction.

## Visual principles
1. One dominant idea per frame.
2. Material clarity: glass, metal and light reveal hierarchy.
3. Editorial scale and deliberate negative space.
4. Spectral restraint: colour is a signal system.
5. System evidence through diagrams, grids and process traces.
6. Accessible contrast over decorative glow.

## Logo direction
Maintain a strong monochrome master mark. Colour/crystal treatment is an expression layer, not the only logo. Required variants: horizontal, compact, monochrome light/dark, small-size simplified mark, app icon, favicon and motion master.

## Typography
Arial is the compatibility baseline. Use tight display tracking, readable body line height and a limited scale. A licensed display face may be evaluated later without making the product dependent on it.

## Image direction
Use premium abstract systems, material studies, product UI and real creative work. Avoid generic robots, AI brains, random neon circuitry, low-quality stock and meaningless gradients.

## Motion
Motion communicates causality, state, continuity and focus. Use fast functional transitions, limited cinematic reveals and complete reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Signature experiences
- Intent becomes a visible structured brief.
- Multiple disciplines assemble around one creative object.
- Candidates remain separate from applied project state.
- Validation and authority are understandable.
- Replay tells a coherent production story.
