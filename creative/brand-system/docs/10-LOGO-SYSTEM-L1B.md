# Logo System L1B — Expressive A Emblem

Status: **candidate; visual approval required**

L1B translates the user-supplied creative `A` reference into manually authored
SVG geometry. It does not replace the restrained L1 family until explicitly
approved.

## Shared chassis

- A-shaped structural body.
- Forward creative ribbon forming the crossbar.
- Circular intelligence module near the upper-right shoulder.
- Dark precision outline shared by all variants.
- Cyan, magenta, violet and amber drawn from approved brand tokens.

## Identity modules

- **Axiom Studio:** camera lens plus pen-nib and media details in the hero tier.
- **Brain X:** connected reasoning graph inside the intelligence module.
- **Imagine X:** expanding generative prism inside the intelligence module.

## Tiers

- **Hero:** full illustrative detail and controlled SVG glow for cinematic and
  marketing placements.
- **Standard:** reduced geometry and no filters for product headers and cards.
- **Micro:** solid shapes, no gradients or filters, suitable for small UI.

## Constraints

Generated lettering and embedded raster images are prohibited. Motion should
animate existing vector groups rather than morphing into an unrelated mark.
Studio-family expansion remains gated on approval of these three prototypes.
