# Channel, Content and Growth System

## Channel roles
### Website
- **Role:** Category education, credibility and conversion
- **Content:** Brand narrative, product proof, use cases, trust architecture and demos
- **KPI:** Qualified demo/start intent, engaged sessions and proof consumption
- **Owner:** Web/Growth

### Product UI
- **Role:** Deliver the brand promise through behaviour
- **Content:** Onboarding, guidance, transparent AI states and reversible actions
- **KPI:** Activation, successful workflows, recovery rate and trust score
- **Owner:** Product

### Documentation
- **Role:** Reduce friction and support expert adoption
- **Content:** Concepts, workflows, architecture, examples and release notes
- **KPI:** Task success, search exits and support deflection
- **Owner:** Developer Experience

### LinkedIn
- **Role:** Enterprise credibility and founder/category thinking
- **Content:** Operating models, case studies, product progress and governance insights
- **KPI:** Qualified engagement, follower quality and inbound conversations
- **Owner:** Founder/Content

### YouTube / Video
- **Role:** Demonstrate workflows and craft
- **Content:** Build films, feature walkthroughs, design breakdowns and launch stories
- **KPI:** Watch quality, completion and assisted conversions
- **Owner:** Content/Motion

### Community
- **Role:** Peer learning, feedback and advocacy
- **Content:** Workflows, critiques, templates, office hours and showcases
- **KPI:** Active contributors, resolved questions and referrals
- **Owner:** Community

### Email
- **Role:** Lifecycle education and relationship continuity
- **Content:** Onboarding, product education, release notes and use-case sequences
- **KPI:** Activation lift, retained readership and conversion
- **Owner:** Lifecycle

### Press / Analyst
- **Role:** Third-party validation and category framing
- **Content:** Company narrative, differentiated architecture, proof and milestones
- **KPI:** Quality coverage, message pull-through and analyst recognition
- **Owner:** Communications

### Events / Workshops
- **Role:** High-trust experiential education
- **Content:** Live systems, governance workshops, build sessions and showcases
- **KPI:** Qualified participation and post-event activation
- **Owner:** Partnerships/Field


## Content pillars
- Creative operating systems and workflow design.
- Human-directed AI and responsible orchestration.
- Design systems, brand systems and production craft.
- Multi-format creation across brand, UI, web, motion, content and automation.
- Build-in-public evidence: decisions, tests, lessons and releases.
- Customer transformations and reusable workflow patterns.

## Funnel
- Discover: category education and distinctive creative work.
- Understand: system explanation, use cases and proof.
- Trust: governance, security, accessibility and implementation evidence.
- Try: guided workflow, template or interactive demonstration.
- Adopt: onboarding, education and support.
- Expand: teams, integrations and advanced disciplines.
- Advocate: showcases, referrals and community teaching.
