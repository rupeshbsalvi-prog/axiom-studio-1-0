# Logo System L1 — Candidate Direction

Status: **candidate; visual approval required**

## Architecture

The family is a branded house derived from the approved Axiom Core geometry.
The outer field and hexagonal lattice remain stable; the inner glyph carries
the product meaning. Colour supports recognition but never replaces geometry.

- Axiom Studio is the masterbrand.
- Brain X is the reasoning and orchestration system.
- Imagine X is the creative direction and generation system.
- Mission Control uses the Axiom Studio mark.
- Axiom AI Studio uses the Imagine X mark pending its naming review.
- Studios use one shared container with discipline-specific glyphs.

## Concept-generation prompt

Use the prompt below in Gemini or ChatGPT only for controlled exploration. Do
not accept generated lettering as a production wordmark.

> Create a premium identity exploration sheet for Axiom Studio, an AI-native
> creative operating system. Preserve this exact visual grammar across every
> mark: circular obsidian field, precise hexagonal Axiom lattice, restrained
> two-weight monoline geometry, optical balance at 16 px, no decorative sci-fi
> detail, no mascots, no mockup backgrounds. Show three related systems: Axiom
> Studio as the neutral master core, Brain X as a violet connected reasoning
> network, and Imagine X as an amber expanding creative spark. Then show the
> same container adapted with simple geometric glyphs for Photo, Vector, Brand,
> Asset Hub, UI/UX, Typography, Presentation, Motion, Video and Whiteboard.
> Symbols only; no generated text, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D rendering,
> no watermark. Present on both Obsidian #090B12 and Paper #F7F6F2 fields.

## Production rules

1. Production symbols are SVG paths and primitives only.
2. Wordmarks are typeset separately; AI-generated lettering is prohibited.
3. Every mark must remain distinguishable in monochrome.
4. Minimum digital symbol size is 16 px; detailed lattice may be removed below
   24 px after the small-size variants are approved.
5. Motion may assemble, awaken and route the mark; it may not continuously spin
   beside reading content.
6. `config/logo.manifest.json` is the candidate identity inventory.

## Approval gate

Approval of L1 locks the architecture, not every optical detail. L2 will add
horizontal wordmarks, monochrome/reversed exports, small-size variants and the
runtime component mapping.
